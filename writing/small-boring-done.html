Small, boring, done

On a new project there's always a pull toward the big system: one dashboard for everything, a process that governs the other processes.

The systems that stuck, on a ship, on a job site and in the shop, were small. Each one had a single owner, a clear input and a check at the end that told you whether it worked. They were boring to describe. In my experience boring means reliable, and once something is reliable people stop thinking about it and get on with the work.
