Nobody wants a newsletter

Nobody wakes up wanting more email. People want to get a little better at something they already care about, like picking a bottle for Saturday or understanding why a wine tastes the way it does.

One good recommendation does more than ten announcements, and an honest "skip this" earns more trust than a month of "don't miss out."

I hold my own newsletter to that bar. If an issue doesn't clear it, I don't send it.
