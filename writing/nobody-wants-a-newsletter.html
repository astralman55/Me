Nobody wants a newsletter

Oct 9, 2026

Nobody wakes up wanting more email. What they want is to be a little better at something they already care about — picking a bottle for Saturday, understanding why a wine tastes the way it does, finding the thing before everyone else does.

One genuinely good recommendation beats ten announcements. One honest “skip this” builds more trust than a month of “don’t miss out.”

That’s the bar I hold my own newsletter to. If an issue doesn’t clear it, it doesn’t go out.
