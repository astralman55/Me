Taste is a dataset

Nov 6, 2026

People say a good shop has "a point of view." In practice that's a few thousand small decisions: this producer over that one, this price point, the region we keep going back to.

You can write those decisions down: grape, region, farming, who made it, why we said yes. Once they're in a structured record they stop living in one person's head, and every new decision has the old ones to check against. The recommendations get better over time, and so do the descriptions and the buying.

A tasting note is structured data dressed up as poetry. I keep the poetry for customers and the structure for myself.
