Taste is a dataset

Nov 6, 2026

People talk about a shop having “a point of view,” as if it were a mood. It isn’t. It’s a few thousand small decisions: this producer and not that one, this price point, this region we keep coming back to.

Decisions can be recorded. Grape, region, farming, who made it, why we said yes. Once they’re structured, they stop living only in one person’s head — and they start compounding. Better recommendations. Better descriptions. Better buying.

A tasting note is structured data pretending to be poetry. That’s fine. Keep the poetry for the customer. Keep the structure for yourself.
