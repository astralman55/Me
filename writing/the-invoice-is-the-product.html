The invoice is the product

Every case of wine comes in with a piece of paper: bottle count, case price, deposit, and discounts that may or may not have been applied. Nobody opens a wine shop because they like that paper.

But the margin lives there. Key in one cost wrong and a good bottle turns into a loss leader, and you won't find out until the quarter closes.

So the first thing I fixed at the shop was receiving. Every invoice gets checked line by line against what the bottle should cost before the wine goes into inventory, and anything that doesn't match gets flagged. It's dull work, which is why I wanted it off my plate.

Automate the job you dread first.

You'll get to the fun work anyway. Errors hide in the work you dread, because a tired person at 6pm is the least reliable part of any business. Hand that job to something that doesn't get tired and spend the hour on the floor, talking to someone about what they're cooking tonight.
