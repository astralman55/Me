Building

The shop, and the small tools that keep it running. Each tool does one job, and most of them exist so nobody has to retype a number.

  • since 2023Harvest Wine ShopAn independent wine shop I founded and run. I handle the buying, licensing, retail and marketing, and I built the tools below to support it.
  • in useInvoice intakeI take a photo of a distributor invoice and it receives the wine into inventory. It checks each line's cost against the bottle price plus deposit and flags anything that doesn't add up.
  • in useProduct launch pipelineTurns an importer's price sheet into finished store listings, with descriptions in the house format and every data field filled in.
  • weeklyCatalog QAWalks the whole catalog looking for missing data and listings that have drifted from the house style. It's a linter for a wine shop.
  • weeklyNewsletter builderAssembles each issue from a template, with every offer checked against the store. I do the editing.
  • ongoingProducer pagesOne page per winery in a consistent format: who they are, where they farm and why we carry them.
  • v1This websitePlain HTML, one stylesheet and a small serverless function for the newsletter signup. No framework.

Principles

1. one job per tool
2. clear input, clear output, a check at the end
3. the store is the source of truth, not a spreadsheet
4. if I dread it, automate it
5. boring is a feature
