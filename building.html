Building

A business, and the small tools that keep it running. Each tool has one job, and most of them exist so a human never has to retype a number.

  • since 2023Harvest Wine ShopThe business itself. An independent wine shop I founded and run: buying, licensing, retail, marketing, and the software that ties it together. Everything below exists to serve it.
  • in useInvoice intakePhoto of a distributor invoice in, received inventory out. Checks every line’s cost against bottle price plus deposit, and flags anything that doesn’t add up.
  • in useProduct launch pipelineTurns an importer price sheet into finished store listings: house-format descriptions, every metadata field filled, nothing left as “TBD.”
  • weeklyCatalog QAAn audit that walks the whole catalog looking for missing data and drift from the house style. The linter for a wine shop.
  • weeklyNewsletter builderBuilds each issue from a template with verified offers and a consistent voice. I edit; it assembles.
  • ongoingProducer pagesA page per winery, in one consistent format. Who they are, where they farm, why we carry them.
  • v1This websitePlain HTML, one stylesheet, one tiny serverless function for the newsletter. No framework.

Principles

1. one job per tool
2. clear input, clear output, a check at the end
3. the store is the source of truth — not a spreadsheet
4. if I dread it, automate it
5. boring is a feature
