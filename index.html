Andy Lowry

Engineer by training. Naval officer, then wine shop founder, now construction management consultant.

I studied ocean engineering at the Naval Academy and spent five years as a Surface Warfare Officer. These days I help deliver construction projects at Accenture, and I run Harvest Wine Shop, which I founded in 2023.

A ship, a job site and a wine shop are all the same problem: a lot of moving parts, not much margin for error, and people who are counting on you. I stood a watch before I ever ran a register. The habits carry over: plan the route, check the work, keep the log.

This is where I keep notes: what I’m writing, reading, building, buying, and throwing away. The formal version is on the resume.
