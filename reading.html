Reading

What I'm reading now, and the books I keep on the shelf.

Books

  • nowThe Practice, by Seth GodinAbout shipping creative work on a schedule. It hit close to home.
  • recentA Philosophy of Software Design, by John OusterhoutDeep modules with simple interfaces. I think about it when I set up processes at the shop.
  • recentAdventures on the Wine Route, by Kermit LynchMy favorite book about buying wine. I reread it every year.
  • recentThe Mom Test, by Rob FitzpatrickHow to ask customers questions they can't lie to you about.
  • shelfThe Pragmatic Programmer, by Hunt & ThomasDon't repeat yourself, and automate whatever you end up repeating.
  • shelfShop Class as Soulcraft, by Matthew B. CrawfordAn argument for work you can put your hands on.
  • shelfTurn the Ship Around!, by L. David MarquetPush authority down to the people who have the information. Marquet learned it commanding a submarine, and it works ashore too.
  • shelfHow Big Things Get Done, by Flyvbjerg & Gardner"Think slow, act fast." Anyone who works on a construction schedule should read it.
