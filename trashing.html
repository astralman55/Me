Trashing

Things, tools and habits I've thrown out and don't miss.

  • Running the shop out of a spreadsheetInventory lives in one system now, so every number is in one place.
  • Tasting notes that say "notes of"Say what it tastes like, or what to cook with it.
  • Selling on points aloneA score is someone else's opinion. People come into the shop for ours.
  • Schedules nobody updatesIf the schedule isn't current, it's wall art.
  • NotificationsAll of them. If something matters, it'll still matter in an hour.
  • Meetings without a decisionIf nothing changes afterward, it was a podcast.
  • "We'll fix it later"In my experience, later means never.
